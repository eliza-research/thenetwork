## 2. Self-Service Gig & Crowdsourced Platforms (Primary — Zero-Touch Agent Hiring)

These platforms let our autonomous agent source, screen, dispatch, verify, and pay NYC workers via API/MCP, web automation, or structured email without a sales call or human account manager.

### 2.1 Craigslist NYC (`newyork.craigslist.org/gigs`) — Primary Volume Engine

Craigslist remains the highest-ROI, zero-markup source of NYC street-team labor, student videographers, and bilingual event activators.

* **Posting Fees (2025–2026):** **$3 to $10 per gig listing** nationally; **New York City sits at $10 per post** across `event gigs (evg)`, `labor gigs (lbg)`, `talent gigs (tlg)`, and `creative gigs (crg)`. Standard employment job boards (`jobs`) cost $35–$75 in NYC and are unnecessary for short-term 4-hour shifts. Each gig post stays live for 30 days with zero per-applicant fees.
* **Category Rules:**
  * Post hand-to-hand fliering shifts in **`evg` (event gigs)** and **`lbg` (labor gigs)** across `mnh` (Manhattan) and `brk` (Brooklyn) sub-areas.
  * Post street-interview creators and charismatic event activators in **`tlg` (talent gigs)** and **`crg` (creative gigs)**.
  * Do not post identical copy twice inside 48 hours; rotate neighborhood focus and headline to prevent automated flagging.
  * Always state the exact hourly pay ($22/hr or $30/hr), shift duration (4 hrs), 18+ age requirement, and W-9/1099 contractor status to comply with Craigslist anti-scam rules and NYLL § 191-d transparency.
* **Email-Relay Automation Workflow:**
  1. Every Craigslist post routes replies through Craigslist's anonymized mail relay (`...@gigs.craigslist.org`) straight into our agent's dedicated inbox (`ops-nyc@ntwrk.party` via webhook/IMAP).
  2. The ad includes a mandatory **human-filter token** (e.g., *"Start your reply with your favorite NYC subway stop + confirm you are 18+ and own a smartphone with GPS camera"*). Our agent auto-rejects any reply missing the token (eliminates ~60% of low-effort spam instantly).
  3. Qualifying applicants receive an automated email with (a) a 3-question async screening rubric, (b) a link to upload a 20-second vertical smartphone intro video (for event activators/creators), and (c) the clickwrap NYLL § 191-d independent contractor agreement + Stripe Connect Express / W-9 onboarding link.
* **Automated Screening Rubric (0–100 Score):**
  * **+25 pts:** Included filter token and confirmed 18+ legal age.
  * **+25 pts:** Resides in NYC 5 boroughs (neighborhood match within 35 min transit of target zone).
  * **+25 pts:** Prior street-team, hospitality, sales, promo, or content experience (keyword + coherence check).
  * **+25 pts:** Completed 20-second phone intro clip showing clear speech and friendly energy (auto-scored by multimodal LLM).
  * **Auto-Hire Threshold:** Score >= 75 moves immediately to shift booking. Score 60–74 goes to the standby bench.
* **Anti-No-Show Protocol:**
  * **Overbook Standby Bench:** For every 2 primary activators scheduled on a shift, book 1 paid-on-activation standby ($15 standby retainer if not called in; full $22/hr if activated at T-2h).
  * **3-Step Automated Check-In:** (1) T-24h SMS/email `"Reply YES to lock tomorrow's 2 PM Union Square shift"`, (2) T-3h confirm, (3) T-15m live GPS selfie at the exact corner landmark before boxes/bundles release. Missing T-3h automatically promotes the standby worker.
  * **Split Payment:** Base shift pay ($22/hr) releases within 24–48 hours of passing automated proof-of-work verification (well inside NYLL § 191-d's 30-day legal ceiling). Verified-signup bonuses settle every Friday.

### 2.2 RentAHuman.ai — Native MCP/API Physical-World Dispatch

RentAHuman (`rentahuman.ai`) is built specifically as an agent-native marketplace to hire verified local humans for physical-world tasks without human intervention.

* **Native MCP & REST API Mechanics:**
  * **Remote MCP Server:** `https://rentahuman.ai/api/mcp` (or local `npx rentahuman-mcp`). Native agent tools support searching profiles by city/coordinates, posting fixed-price bounties, messaging applicants, inspecting submitted evidence, and releasing escrow.
  * **REST API:** `https://rentahuman.ai/api` authenticated with `X-API-Key: rah_...`. Filter NYC talent via `city=New York` or latitude/longitude bounding boxes (e.g., Manhattan/Williamsburg).
* **Pricing & Fees (2025–2026):**
  * **$0 monthly subscription.** Pay only when funding a task.
  * **18% buyer service fee** added on top of worker payout (e.g., a $50 worker task costs $59 total; the worker keeps 100% of the $50). Minimum worker payout is $1 USD.
  * **Payment Rails:** Fiat via Stripe Checkout (USD) or USDC on Base. *(Note: US consumer/worker payouts stay strictly on fiat/Stripe unless a future explicit policy flag enables crypto.)*
* **Escrow & Dispute Windows:**
  1. Agent funds task amount + 18% fee upfront into escrow before listing goes live.
  2. NYC worker claims task, executes on site, and uploads required proof artifacts (timestamped geo photos, delivery receipt, QR scan count).
  3. **4-day automated review window:** Our agent inspects proof artifacts deterministically. Valid proof triggers automatic escrow release; unfilled tasks or rejected non-performance refund unused escrow to the wallet.
* **Best Role Fit in Month 1:**
  * **Print-Box Logistics:** Picking up boxed 4×6" flier batches from 4over4 (Astoria, Queens) or Printleaf (Midtown) and handing them off to street-team leads at Union Square or Williamsburg ($35–$55 per courier run).
  * **Rapid Solo Spot Drops:** 2-hour high-footfall solo fliering or event-line distribution when a Craigslist worker cancels last minute ($50–$60 payout + 18% fee).
  * **Spot Audits:** Paying an independent NYC worker $15–$20 to walk past an active agency street team and submit two timestamped verification photos confirming the agency crew is actually on station and handing out fliers.

## 5. NYC Legal & Compliance Rules for Fliering, Street Teams & Filming

All autonomous hiring briefs, clickwrap contracts, and automated proof checks enforce these exact statutes as hard guardrails.

### 5.1 Anti-Posting Ban vs. Legal Hand-to-Hand Distribution

* **STRICTLY PROHIBITED — NYC Administrative Code § 10-119 ("Posting"):** It is illegal to paste, tape, glue, staple, nail, or attach fliers, stickers, or posters to lampposts, utility poles, trees, traffic signals, mailboxes, trash cans, or any city street furniture.
  * **Penalties (NYC Admin Code § 10-121 / OATH-ECB):** **$75–$150** for a first offense; **$150–$250+** for repeat offenses within 12 months, plus city removal costs and extra fines for damaging trees.
  * **Hard Agent Rule:** Every street-team brief explicitly forbids posting/taping fliers anywhere and forbids placing fliers on parked car windshields (NYC Admin Code § 10-119 / VTL littering enforcement). Violation forfeits shift bonus and triggers immediate contractor block.
* **PROTECTED — Hand-to-Hand Distribution on Public Sidewalks (First Amendment):** Handing fliers directly to willing pedestrians on public NYC sidewalks is protected speech and **does not require a permit**.
  * **Sidewalk Clearance Rule:** Keep at least **half the sidewalk width (minimum 8 feet)** clear at all times. Never block building doorways, crosswalk ramps, bus stops, or subway stairways. Never follow or harass pedestrians who decline a flier. Pick up any dropped fliers within 15 feet (anti-litter compliance).

### 5.2 Restricted Zones (MTA Subways, NYC Parks & Private Property)

* **MTA Subway System (21 NYCRR § 1050.6):**
  * **Commercial flyers are BANNED outright** inside all MTA subway stations, platforms, trains, turnstile paid-fare zones, and underground mezzanines under **21 NYCRR § 1050.6(b)** ("free distribution of promotional goods or materials" = prohibited commercial activity without an MTA license).
  * **Hard Agent Rule:** Street teams MUST stay strictly on **outdoor public city sidewalks at least 15–20 feet outside** subway stair entrances and never step down into mezzanines or stations.
* **NYC Parks Regulations (56 RCNY § 1-05 & 20-Person Rule):**
  * Small-scale walking hand-to-hand distribution (<20 people, no tables, no tents, no amplified sound) on park perimeter sidewalks does not require a Parks Special Event Permit.
  * Any organized gathering of **more than 20 people**, any **amplified sound**, or any **table/tent/structure** in a NYC park requires a NYC Parks Special Event Permit. Never place tables, pop-up banners, or speakers in Washington Square Park, Union Square Park, or Bryant Park.
* **Private Property & POPS:** Privately Owned Public Spaces (POPS), Bryant Park Corporation interior lawns, Hudson Yards plaza, Grand Central Terminal, and indoor malls/food halls enforce private rules. Stay on public city sidewalks unless invited as an event guest/sponsor.

### 5.3 Public Sidewalk Filming & MOME Permit Thresholds

* **NYC Mayor's Office of Media and Entertainment (MOME):** A film permit is **NOT required** on public NYC sidewalks when the crew uses **only a handheld camera (or single tripod) plus handheld mic/props**, does not assert exclusive use of the sidewalk, leaves >= 8 feet / half-sidewalk clearance, and uses zero ground lighting stands, C-stands, dollies, or production parking.
* **Hard Agent Rule for Creators:** Handheld smartphone/mirrorless camera + wireless clip-on lavalier or shotgun mic ONLY. Zero light stands, zero C-stands, zero cones or ropes.

### 5.4 Likeness & Commercial Release Law (CRITICAL — NY Civil Rights Law §§ 50–51)

* **The Rule:** Under **New York Civil Rights Law §§ 50 and 51**, using a person's name, portrait, picture, or voice for **advertising or trade purposes** without **prior written consent** is an actionable civil tort (and misdemeanor under § 50) carrying statutory injunctions, compensatory damages, and exemplary (punitive) damages for willful violations.
* **Why Street Interviews Require Written Releases:** Editorial news enjoys a First Amendment exception, **but a brand-sponsored street interview promoting The Network / `slop.date` / `peon.biz` / `friends.help` is commercial advertising.** A casual verbal "sure, film me" is **NOT sufficient** under NY Civil Rights Law § 51!
* **Zero-Friction Digital Release Workflow:**
  * Every creator carries a laminated QR card linking to a 10-second mobile release form (`ntwrk.party/release?creator=<id>&shoot=<id>`).
  * Before or immediately after an interviewee appears on camera, the interviewee enters full legal name, email/phone, checks the 18+ confirmation box, checks the NY Civil Rights Law § 51 commercial-use consent box, and taps **Sign** (timestamp + IP + optional quick selfie logged to `marketing.proofs`).
  * Any bystander or interviewee who does **not** sign the mobile release MUST be blurred or cut from the final video before publication. Our automated proof verifier checks `likeness_releases_count >= featured_interviewees_count`.

### 5.5 NY Freelance Labor Law & 18+ Platform Guardrails

* **NYLL § 191-d — New York Statewide "Freelance Isn't Free Act" (FIFA):**
  * Applies to any independent contractor engagement (or aggregated engagements with the same worker within 120 days) valued at **$800 or more**, and our agent applies it uniformly to **all** direct 1099 contracts starting at $1 to prevent aggregation traps.
  * **Mandatory Written Contract:** Must state itemized services, pay rate, payment method, and exact payment due date. Retain signed clickwrap records for **6 years**.
  * **Mandatory Payout SLA:** Pay on or before the contract date, and **never later than 30 days** after service completion (our internal SLA is **<= 15 days**, with target payout in **48 hours** after proof verification). Violations carry double damages and attorney's fees.
* **Strict 18+ Floor (`inv_adult_only_paid`):**
  * **Every paid street-team worker, event activator, creator, and filmed commercial interviewee MUST be 18+** (age verified via government ID / Stripe Connect KYC / release birth-year check). No person under 18 or with unknown age is ever hired, filmed for ads, or paid.
