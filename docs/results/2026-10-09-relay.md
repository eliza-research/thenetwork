# Relay through the agent and the photo in the probe (2026-10-09)

Critical path item 7, engine side (docs/mvp-plan.md; PRD 40.5; AGENTS.md founder decision 9). After a mutual yes, members never talk directly: every text, "send them my number" and photo goes through the agent, with consent per item. The first anonymous probe can carry one photo of an adult who consented. This report gives the design, the gate results and the integration notes for the platform session.

- Code: `packages/engine/src/relay.ts` (new), `packages/engine/src/packs/slop/plan.ts` (`probePhotoRefs`), `packages/engine/src/packs/slop/copy.ts` (`slopProbeMessage`), `packages/sim/src/apps/slop/relay.ts` (new, the relay in the slop world), `packages/sim/src/apps/slop/world.ts` and `snapshot.ts` (the relay and photo arms call the engine functions), `scripts/sim/relay.ts` (new block), `scripts/sim/slop.ts` (relay world gates; the pinned world now uses the engine relay).
- Corpora (hand-written): `evals/relay/relay.jsonl` (160 rows), `evals/relay/relay-paraphrases.jsonl` (65 rows), `evals/relay/requests.jsonl` (37 rows).
- Hand-written: this report, the rules, the corpora and the sim templates. Generated: every number below.

## 1. Design

### 1.1 Photo in the probe

`probePhotoRefs(subject, recipient)` (packs/slop/plan.ts) returns the photo references a probe may carry. It returns `[]` unless all of these are true:

- the subject and the recipient are adults: core `canBeMatched` on the LOWEST stated age (an unknown age fails closed);
- the subject consented to show their photo to a match (`photoConsent`; separate from the upload consent, which today says "No other member sees them");
- the subject is not on a safety hold;
- the photo id is opaque (`isOpaquePhotoId`: 8-64 characters of `[A-Za-z0-9_-]`, no run of 7+ digits, not a URL). A probe never carries a URL or anything with personal data.

At most one photo (`PROBE_PHOTO_MAX = 1`). `slopProbeMessage(probe, refs)` adds one fixed sentence before the question ("I've attached a photo they chose to share; their name and number stay private until you both say yes.") and returns `{ text, photos }`. With no refs, the text is unchanged. The probe text from the core (`buildProbe`, the leak gate) does not change, so `slopProbeText` and the pack's `probeText` hook stay as they were; the copy no longer says "no photo".

The slop world uses the same function: in `world.ts`, a probe in the photo arm carries the other person's photo only when `probePhotoRefs` returns a ref (sim ids from `simPhotoIds`, consent from `simPhotoConsent`, share `photos.consent`, default 1). The reveal back-out reduction from the photo applies only to a member whose probe carried it. With the default consent share of 1 the photo arm behaves as before.

### 1.2 The relay policy

`relayItem(item, ctx)` returns `{ decision: "pass" | "hold" | "block", reasons, rendered, photos, record, senderNotice }`. `rendered` is the agent's wording and the only thing the recipient sees ("" unless the decision is pass):

| Kind | Rendered |
|---|---|
| text | `Sam says: "<their words>"` |
| photo | `Sam sent you a photo.` (or with a caption) plus the photo ids |
| contact_share | `Sam asked me to send you their number: +12125550147. Say "send them my number" if you want me to send yours back.` |

Every check runs; the most severe outcome wins (block > hold > pass). Block = never delivered. Hold = staff review queue (the platform may release it).

| Check | Outcome | Reason codes |
|---|---|---|
| Sender and recipient are the opportunity's two participants | block | `party:not_participant` |
| Either side is a minor or has an unknown age (lowest stated age, core `canBeMatched`) | block | `minor:party` |
| A block edge in either direction | block | `party:blocked` |
| Recipient (or sender) opted out (STOP, leave, pause, deleted) | block | `optout:*` |
| A safety hold on either side | hold | `safety:*` |
| Opportunity not `mutual`, or one side has not said yes | block | `state:*` |
| Contact share: explicit consent for this item, by the sender, within 15 minutes, before the item; a valid phone or email from the person record; no free text; once per match | block | `consent:*`, `contact_share:*` |
| Photo: explicit send naming these photo ids, the sender's photo consent, ids the sender owns, opaque ids, 1-3 photos | block | `consent:*`, `photo:*` |
| Core LeakGuard on the text and the sender's last 4 delivered texts (split numbers): other members' private facts | hold | `leak:<hash>` |
| Canary | block | `canary:<hash>` |
| Contact patterns in free text (core guard plus spelled-out phone numbers) | hold | `contact:*` |
| Rating, score or attractiveness wording ("how hot did you rate me?", "what's my score", "out of your league", "appearance:face=...") | hold | `rating:*` |
| A minor's self-disclosure ("I'm 16", "still in high school") | block, `ageSignal: true` | `minor:signal` |
| Money requests, gift cards, crypto and "investments" | hold | `scam:*` |
| Moving off-platform before the first date (`metAt` unset) | hold | `offplatform:*` |
| Fishing for contact, address, workplace or last name | hold | `fishing:*` |
| Prompt injection aimed at the agent | hold | `injection` |
| Insults, sexual pressure | hold | `harass:*` |
| Threats, slurs | block | `harass_severe:*` |
| Rate limits per sender per match: 6 per 10 minutes, 40 per day, 6 photos per day | hold | `rate:*` |
| Optional LLM hook (`relayItemAsync`), only on items the rules pass; an error holds unless `failOpen` | hold | `llm:*` |

The sender's own private facts are excluded from the leak check (`exceptOwner`): a member may say "I have two kids" about themselves. Private facts of the other member (or anyone else), canaries and score tags are not.

The rules run on core `textVariants` (lowercase, NFKC, homoglyphs, spaced letters, leetspeak). The scam and harassment rules are written for relayed chat between two matched adults; they are separate from `packages/network/src/classify.ts`, which reads messages to the agent and is not importable from the engine.

`senderNotice` tells the sender what happened in general words and never repeats the matched text: for contact details it points to "send them my number"; for rating questions it says "I can't share anything like that".

`parseRelayRequest(text)` reads a member's request: `contact_share`, `photo`, `text` (with the body of "tell them I'm running late") or `none`. A hedged, negated or question request ("should I send my number?", "don't send them my number", "after the date") is `none`. The platform turns `contact_share` or `photo` into an item with a `RelayConsent` for that item id.

### 1.3 The relay log

`record` is the log row: `itemId, opportunityId, app, kind, from, to, at, decision, reasons, photoCount, contactShared, ageSignal, policy`. No message body, no contact value, no photo id. Reasons are codes from a fixed list (`RELAY_REASON_FAMILIES`); any match-specific part is a keyed hash (core `labelHash`, `LEAK_LABEL_KEY` in production). `pastContacts(records, member)` lists everyone who exchanged a delivered item with a member, for ban notices (PRD 40.5, 40.7).

### 1.4 Alternatives considered

1. **A persistent free thread with only the leak guard** (forward everything, block contact patterns). Simpler, but it has no consent per item, no rating or scam rules and no state check; a contact share would be a free-text number. Rejected: PRD 40.5 asks for consent per item and a scam classifier.
2. **LLM-first classification of every relayed item** (gpt-6-luna judge on each message). Better on paraphrases, but a model call per message, non-deterministic, not runnable in `bun run sim`, and a model outage would stop the relay. Kept as the optional hook after the rules, so the rules stay the deterministic floor.
3. **One-shot number swap only** (no thread; after the mutual yes the agent swaps numbers on both "yes"). Least code, but it moves the pair off-platform at once, so the scam check, the relay log and ban notices have nothing to see. Prototype P7 decides between a thread and a one-shot swap; this code supports both (a contact share is one item kind).

## 2. Simulation: the relay in the slop world

`packages/sim/src/apps/slop/relay.ts`. After each reveal, both members send items through `relayItem` with roles from the world: honest members (1-3 messages, a contact share with p 0.25, a photo with p 0.12, and with p 0.05 a contact share whose consent covers another item, which must block), romance scammers (one script item per oracle harm: off-platform, money, gift card or crypto), harassers (one item per harassment harm), age liars (a slip with p 0.35) and two sim-only roles on 3% of honest adults each (contact fishers, rating probers). A harm whose script item is not delivered does not happen. A sender with an item held or blocked for scam, off-platform, harassment, injection or a minor signal goes on a safety hold (cleared for honest members by the 3-day review, as in the iteration-2 model). Contact with a hidden minor is not a relay item and is not prevented by the relay.

The pinned world (`scripts/sim/slop.ts`, seeds 13-16, 4 weeks, 300 per city) now runs `relay: { engine: true }` instead of the iteration-2 assumption (scam recall 0.85, hostile recall 0.7, 0.5% false positives).

## 4. Integration notes for the platform

Owner: the platform session (`packages/network`, `packages/platform`, `deploy`). Everything below is imported from `@thenetwork/engine`.

**Photo in the probe** (probe send path, `packages/network/src/network.ts` where the probe text is built):

1. After `attention.buildProbe(...)` returns a probe for a slop recipient, call `probePhotoRefs({ age: lowestAge(other), photoConsent, photoIds, held }, { age: lowestAge(recipient) })`.
   - `photoIds`: the other person's photo rows (`platform.photos`) with the current consent, best first, by row id. The row id must be opaque (`isOpaquePhotoId`).
   - `photoConsent`: a NEW consent ("show my photo to a proposed match"). The current `PHOTO_CONSENT` text says "No other member sees them", so it does not cover this; it needs a new version and the founder's approval of the copy.
2. Call `slopProbeMessage(probe, refs)` and send `text` with the image for `photos[0]`, resolved at send time to a short-lived signed link or an MMS attachment. Never put a URL or a storage key in the probe record or the log; store the photo id.
3. Run the leak guard on `text` as today (the added sentence is fixed and carries nothing).

**Relay** (inbound path after the mutual yes, before the outbound queue):

1. On an inbound message from a member with a mutual opportunity, call `parseRelayRequest(text)`. For `contact_share` or `photo`, create the item id, record a `RelayConsent { kind, by: sender, itemId, at: now, photoIds }`, and build the item (`contact` from the person's verified login phone, never from the message). For `text`, the body is the item text. Anything else stays a message to the agent.
2. Build the `RelayContext`: `now` from the Clock; the opportunity (`status: "mutual"` only after both yeses; `metAt` after the date); both parties with the LOWEST age, `optedOut` (STOP, leave slop, pause), `held` (safety holds and bans), `photoIds` and `photoConsent`; `blocked` from block edges both ways after alias resolution; `history` = the relay log rows of this opportunity; `recentTexts` = the sender's last 4 delivered texts in this thread; `guard` = `relayGuard({ facts: both members' agent_private and matchable facets with owner, canaries })`, built once per thread.
3. Call `relayItem(item, ctx)` (or `relayItemAsync` with a hook, if one is approved).
4. **pass**: enqueue `rendered` (and the photos) to the recipient through the outbound queue (the queue's leak check still runs; for a contact share pass the shared value as `allow`). Reply `senderNotice` to the sender.
5. **hold**: put the original text in the staff review queue (access-controlled, deleted after review), reply `senderNotice`. If a reason family is `scam`, `offplatform`, `harass`, `injection` or `minor`, put the SENDER on a safety hold pending review.
6. **block**: reply `senderNotice`; deliver nothing. If `record.ageSignal`, record the stated age (the lowest age wins; core policy), which ends matching and relay for that person.
7. Store `record` in a `relay_log` table (append-only; no body) and `threadMessage(result)` in the thread table (the rendered text only for delivered items). Use `pastContacts` from the log for ban notices (critical path item 8).
8. Every proactive part of this is not proactive: relay items are member-initiated, so they do not count against the 3-a-day person cap. STOP still stops everything.

Suggested tables: `relay_log(item_id pk, opportunity_id, app, kind, from_person, to_person, at, decision, reasons text[], photo_count, contact_shared, age_signal, policy)` and `relay_thread(opportunity_id, item_id, from_person, to_person, at, kind, decision, rendered null, photo_ids text[], reasons text[])`.

## 5. Limits and open points

- The rules are hand-written and were tuned on the hand-written corpora (section 3 gives the first pass on the paraphrase file before tuning). They will miss new scam scripts. The pilot needs a real held-out set (prototype P7), and the LLM hook is the planned second layer.
- The sim templates are the simulator's own; recall on them is an upper bound. The corpus gates are the better measure.
- A core guard gap: a phone number spelled in words with commas ("three four seven, five five five, ...") passed `LeakGuard`. The relay adds its own spelled-number check (`spelledPhone`); the core guard owner may want the same fix for every outbound message.
- Photo consent for probes and relay is a new consent version the platform must add (section 4).
