## Iteration 2: the founder's decisions of 2026-10-08 (current defaults, plans-v1.1.0)

The founder decided: (1) **a separate plan allowance**: 1 initial plan invite per member per 7 days, on top of attention's 2/7d intro cap (unchanged), only for members with a stated, standing or learned window or the opt-in weekly check-in; quiet hours, the unanswered rules and Blooio limits still apply; (2) **crews after one great plan**: if at least 3 attendees say they would do it again, a recurring crew is offered and each person opts in; (3) **keep the familiarity term**. All three are the new defaults (`DEFAULT_PLANS`, `plans-v1.1.0`). The 8-seed (30 days) and 60-day (now also 8 seeds) comparisons were rerun with them, with the no-check-in and no-window-priming arms, and with the iteration-1 behaviour (plan invites on the intro cap) for comparison. Same simulator, seeds 1-8, keys empty, no LLM calls, $0.

### Result

- **With the founder defaults the planner adds value at 60 days: met + worthwhile +8.4 ± 2.4 per seed (32.8 → 41.1, about 3.5 standard errors), V14 +9.0 ± 0.8 points (26.4% → 35.5%), adults with no value event -10.7 ± 2.2 points (42.7% → 32.0%), Gini 0.632 → 0.561.** Plans add +11.8 ± 1.0 met + worthwhile meetings per seed and intros lose -3.4 ± 2.1. At 30 days: +3.5 ± 2.2 met + worthwhile (not significant), V14 +6.1 ± 1.5, no-value share -9.5 ± 2.1.
- **The separate allowance changes little in this simulator.** Against plan invites on the intro cap (iteration 1): 30 days -0.1 ± 2.2 met + worthwhile, 60 days +1.3 ± 2.8 (intros +1.9 ± 2.7), V14 equal. It costs +0.06 interruptions per member-week (separate plan messages instead of plans riding in an intro message). Intros still lose meetings because a member waiting on a plan answer, or booked into a plan, gets no new item until it resolves (one open question at a time), not because of the cap. The allowance is shipped because the founder decided it; the measured reason to keep it is that no intro is ever displaced from the cap by a plan (0 plan invites on the intro cap).
- **Crews after one great plan form rarely: 0.25 per seed in 30 and in 60 days** (0.5 / 0.75 sessions). The bottleneck is upstream: held plans average 2.2 attendees, so few plans have 3 attendees who all would do it again (2-3 members offered per seed; 70% x (1 - ignore probability) opt in, so a 3-person offer forms a crew about 1 time in 4).
- **The two assumptions still carry the result.** Without the weekly check-in (standing availability only): 60 days -3.0 ± 2.1 met + worthwhile vs the baseline (-11.4 ± 2.0 vs P), V14 +1.7. Without window priming: 60 days +3.4 ± 1.9 (-5.0 ± 1.9 vs P), V14 +7.0. Window priming matters less than in iteration 1 (30 days: -1.0 ± 1.2 vs P, iteration 1: -3.5 ± 1.6); the reason was not isolated.
- **Invariants hold in every arm:** 0 declared minors in any plan role, 0 minor contacts, 0 canary leaks, 0 names in plan probes, **0 sends over the intro cap and 0 over the plan allowance**, 0 quiet-hour sends (check-ins included), 0 messages past Blooio's third unanswered, 0 judge invariants.

### What was built (iteration 2)

- `config.ts`: `plans.allowance { enabled: true, limit: 1, periodDays: 7 }`; `plans.crews.minPlans` 1 (default; 2 = the previous two-plan rule); `plans-v1.1.0`. Attention's caps (`DEFAULT_ATTENTION.caps`) are unchanged.
- `plans.ts`: `planAllowanceEligible(evidence, checkInOptIn, now)` (stated window this week, standing window, learned accepted / attended time, or the opt-in check-in); `planAllowanceConfig(att, pcfg)` (attention config for composing a plan message: every state's cap = the allowance, one plan per message with outside-world companions, no break-ins); `crewOptIn(crew, optedIn)`; `detectCrews` with `minPlans` 1.
- Harness (`packages/engine/experiments/plansNetwork.ts`, since deleted): a plan lane. Plan items of eligible members are held apart and sent once a day inside the member's send window through `attention.composeMessage` with `planAllowanceConfig` and a ledger of that member's plan invites only; the member's only-when-asked state (full ledger, stub unanswered count), quiet hours and the Blooio conversation streak are the same as for intros. The intro composer sees the ledger without plan invites, so its 2/7d cap is exactly as before. Crew offers ride in the reply to the post-plan answer; each proposed crew is offered once.
- Harness only: a member offered a crew opts in with p = 0.7 x (1 - ignore probability), per crew.
- Tests (in the engine test suite at the time, since deleted; now validated by `bun run sim`): allowance eligibility, the allowance config (1/7d, intro cap unchanged, one plan per message), the gates that still apply (quiet hours, two-unanswered, Blooio streak, paused, minors), crew opt-in, `minPlans` 1 and 2 (`plans.test.ts`, 28 tests); `plans-sim.test.ts` checks at most 1 plan invite and at most 2 intro invites per member per 7 days end to end.

### Defaults (plans-v1.1.0)

- `allowance { enabled: true, limit: 1, periodDays: 7 }`; `crews.minPlans: 1` (offer after one plan with >= 3 attendees who would do it again; each opts in; >= 3 opt-ins form the crew); familiarity term on (+0.05 one familiar face, -0.10 clique).
- Unchanged from iteration 1 (`plans-v1.0.0`):
  - Horizon: starts 72 hours to 7 days ahead; planner runs Monday and Thursday 09:00 local; 6 plans per city per run; one plan per member per run; 5-day member cooldown.
  - Demand: a stated (check-in), standing or learned window with P(free) >= 0.5; activity fit 1 (stated interest, skill or want); family-only fit (0.5) does not qualify.
  - Group: invite 4-6 (activity-partner plans 2, only when no group clears the floors), quorum 3 (2), beam width 8, 3 alternates; U = 0.6 min + 0.4 mean; floors min u_i >= 0.40, U >= 0.40; venue in a member's area 1, elsewhere 0.85.
  - Lifecycle: probe deadline the earlier of 96 hours after the plan is made and 30 hours before the start; late joins until 6 hours before; a yes does not hold the member (`yesHolds: false`); the reveal is the booked plan; fallbacks on (smaller, solo event, next week with +0.1 fit for 10 days).
  - Plan item: `plan_probe`, review before the first probe. Stated windows: confidence 0.8, P(free) inside 0.85, outside the stated week x 0.6. Crews weekly, hand-off after 3 sessions. `unservedOnly: false`, `partnerPlans: true`.

### Integration asks for the network session (changes from iteration 1)

1. **Plan allowance.** Hold plan items of eligible members (`plans.planAllowanceEligible`) apart from the intro hold queue. Once per day in the member's send window, compose a plan message with `attention.composeMessage({ ..., ledger: <this member's plan invites only>, cfg: plans.planAllowanceConfig(attentionCfg) })` and the member's normal `MemberAttention` view (its `onlyWhenAsked` must come from the full ledger and the two-unanswered state). Record the ledger entry as a plan invite: it never counts toward the intro cap (filter plan invites out of the ledger passed to the intro composer), but it does count toward two-unanswered and the Blooio streak. Members who are not eligible get plan items on the intro cap as before.
2. **One plan per message;** event or place suggestions may ride along.
3. **Crews.** After the post-plan answers, call `detectCrews` (minPlans 1). Offer each proposed crew once, in the reply to the member's answer ("want to make this a weekly thing with the same people?"). Call `crewOptIn(crew, yesIds)`; below 3 there is no crew and nobody learns who declined.
4. Everything else in the iteration-1 list still applies (capture, planner run, review, probes, booking, fallbacks, after the plan, venues, judge invariants); see the iteration-1 text in git history at `16cde70`. The ConsentNetwork wiring of these asks is summarized in [SUMMARY.md](SUMMARY.md) ("Network capital events and plans v1.1").

## Iteration 1 (summary; plans-v1.0.0, superseded defaults)

- The planner was built time-first (stated or inferred window, a liked activity, a public venue or listed event, 4-6 person groups by least misery, quorum 3, backfill, fallbacks, crews after two plans) with plan invites on the 2/7d intro cap. Every invariant held.
- 30 days, 8 seeds, against attention v1.2 "(c)": V14 +7.1 ± 1.4 points (28.4% to 35.5%), adults with no value event -8.3 ± 1.0 points, met + worthwhile +3.5 ± 2.1 per seed (15.0 to 18.5, not significant). Quorum was reached by 23% of probed plans.
- Two harness assumptions carried most of the gain: the weekly check-in and window priming. The founder then decided a separate plan allowance, crews after one great plan, and keeping the familiarity term (iteration 2).
