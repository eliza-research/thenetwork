# Plans: "I'm free Saturday night and into X" (Phase 2), measured (2026-10-08)

Spec: [docs/design/2026-10-07-experience-design.md](../design/2026-10-07-experience-design.md) section 4 (plans), section 1.11 (availability capture), and [growth doc section 3](../design/2026-10-07-growth-capital-ownership.md) (MVP light touch: public venues only, no home hosting, no money). Builds on [2026-10-07-attention-budget.md](2026-10-07-attention-budget.md) iterations 3-4 ("attention v1.2"). **No LLM calls, $0.** Every run and test was made with the keys empty (`OPENAI_API_KEY= SURPLUS_API_KEY= CEREBRAS_API_KEY=`) and without `LIVE_TESTS`. Tables below are generated by the command shown; the text is hand-written.

## Result in one paragraph

The planner is built as specified (time-first: members with a stated or inferred window, an activity they like, a public venue or listed event, a 4-6 person group scored by least misery with a familiarity term, 2-person activity-partner plans, quorum 3, backfill, three fallbacks, crews) and it keeps every invariant: 0 declared minors in any plan role, 0 minor contacts, 0 canary leaks, 0 names in a plan probe, 0 over-cap sends, 0 quiet-hour sends (check-ins included). Against the baseline (v1.2 engine + attention v1.2 "(c)", the attention results' HQ-c arm, reproduced exactly: 18 18 10 15 13 18 13 15), the planner with check-in and standing-availability capture **spreads value: V14 +7.1 ± 1.4 points (28.4% → 35.5%), the share of adults with no value event in 30 days −8.3 ± 1.0 points (56.1% → 47.8%), Gini of value events 0.686 → 0.637**. Met + worthwhile rises **+3.5 ± 2.1 per seed (15.0 → 18.5)**, which is not significant over 8 seeds: plans add +5.5 ± 0.6 met + worthwhile meetings per seed but take −2.0 ± 2.0 from intros, because plan probes use the same 2-per-week cap (+0.23 interruptions per member-week, all within cap). Meetings per interruption are unchanged (0.061 vs 0.064). 17% of members who got any value got it first from a plan. Two harness assumptions carry most of the gain and must be checked with real members: **(1) the weekly check-in** (without it, standing availability alone gives −1.1 ± 1.0 met + worthwhile and only +2.5 points of V14) and **(2) window priming**, that a member who said "free Saturday" answers a matching plan like a request they made (without it: +0.0 ± 1.7 met + worthwhile, V14 +4.1). Quorum is reached by 23% of probed plans and 65% of confirmed seats are filled, against the design's sim targets of 50% and 75%.

## How it was measured

```bash
OPENAI_API_KEY= SURPLUS_API_KEY= CEREBRAS_API_KEY= bun packages/engine/experiments/plans.ts                     # 30 days, seeds 1-8, every variant
OPENAI_API_KEY= SURPLUS_API_KEY= CEREBRAS_API_KEY= bun packages/engine/experiments/plans.ts --days 60 --seeds 1,2,3,4 --only "^(B|P|P-crew1|P-nofam) "
```

Simulator: 150 personas, 30 days (and 60 days on 4 seeds), discrete mode, engine-v1.2.0 defaults with the attention supply budget, v1.2 snapshot features, history fed back, attention-v1.2.0, plans-v1.0.0 (config hash `752f8f2077f49d76`). Model: none (no LLM calls). The simulated reviewer approves every plan (the stub has no review, as in the attention results). Differences in brackets are paired by seed against the baseline with the same fidelity fixes, mean ± standard error over 8 seeds; a difference under about 2 standard errors is noise.

**Baseline (B).** The attention results' HQ-c arm: N3 (rolling 12:00 learned send time, always probe first, partner probe in the partner's send window, only initial invites on the cap, time options) with the booked-plan reveal "(c)", fidelity fixes 1-3 (ask-primed probes, outside-world events as companions, time-dependent attendance). It runs through the plans harness network with the planner off and reproduces the published per-seed values exactly. B0 / P0 are the same without the fixes (Q-c: 11 18 8 8 10 14 9 8, also exact).

**Metrics.** Met + worthwhile: held meetings (>= 2 came) where everyone who came had enjoyment >= 0.5 (intros: the sim's oracle; plans: the plan oracle below, least misery). V14, no-value share, Gini: from value events (every attendee of a held meeting, plus acted-on event suggestions), over adults. Interruptions: delivered proactive messages (check-ins are not proactive). Quorum rate: plans booked / plans whose probe went out. Attendance: seats where the member came / confirmed seats (booked-plan reveals). Plan precision: plans held with >= 3 who came, mean >= 0.55, min >= 0.40 (design 4.9 label). First value from a plan: share of members with any value event whose earliest one was a plan.

### Harness-only approximations (documented, not engine behaviour)

1. **Plan oracle** (`plansHarness.planEnjoyment`): e_i = clamp01(0.85 x A x C x L + noise). A = 0.4 + 0.6 x like_i(activity), with like = 1 for a held interest or skill, 0.8 for a held want the activity serves, 0.35 for a liked family, 0.1 otherwise. C = clamp(0.35 + mean_j [sim pairEnjoyment(i, j) + the sim's pair chemistry draw], 0.2, 1.1): group chemistry from the simulator's own pair model. L = 1 - 0.06 x (group size over preferred size) x (1 - social energy) - 0.05 if the venue is not in the member's home or work area + 0.08 x (1 - social energy) if someone they know is there. Noise N(0, 0.08) per plan and member. The sim still records its own enjoyment for plan meetings (used by persona feedback); the metrics replace it with this oracle.
2. **Plan probe answers** (`planYesProb`, installed outermost on `oracle.probe` for plan keys): (0.3 + 0.7 x this week's capacity) x fatigue x (0.15 + 0.75 x like) x (0.6 + 0.4 x social energy) x size comfort x presence. **Window priming:** a member whose check-in answer this week covers the plan's time answers 0.85 x (0.3 + 0.7 x like), the plan analogue of fix 1's ask priming. The persona agent still decides whether to ignore the message. A yes at a time the member is not actually free (hidden weekly availability, attention iteration 3) becomes "can't make that time" (a no, recorded as `declined_time`).
3. **Capture.** Check-in opt-in with p = 0.25 + 0.35 x social energy (about 45%); the answer lists the candidate slots of the next 7 days the persona is truly free for with p = 0.8, plus busy slots with p = 0.05. Standing availability stated at onboarding by 50% of members: their free evenings (17-22) and each weekend day (10-17) with p = 0.6. The persona agent decides whether to answer the check-in at all; its text is not parsed.
4. **Attendance** comes from the simulator: the booked-plan reveal is a named proposal the persona decides on (probe-primed), then the sim's flakiness and fix 3 (a seat at a time the member is not free does not come with p = 0.7).
5. **Post-plan answers** for crews: who came (the persona's own record) and "anyone you'd do this again with?" = attendees with plan enjoyment >= 0.6.
6. **Venues:** a synthetic list, 3 public venues per activity per city in rotating neighbourhoods, open 9-23 (day activities 8-18). Listed events are the snapshot's `publicEvents` (6 per city per week).
7. Personas answer one item per message (attention results, approximation): a digest that carries a plan and an intro gets an answer on one of them only, which penalises plans that ride along with an intro.

## What the numbers say

- **Plans spread value; they do not yet add much of it.** V14 (+7.1 ± 1.4), the no-value share (-8.3 ± 1.0) and the Gini (-0.05 ± 0.01) move by 5-8 standard errors; met + worthwhile moves by 1.7. Plans reach members intros do not: 17% of members' first value was a plan (44% without the fidelity fixes, where intros are weaker).
- **The cap is the constraint.** A group plan needs 3 yeses inside its probing window from members who each have 2 initial invites per week. The largest single reason for a "no" is a probe never sent before the deadline (49 of 118 per seed: the member was at cap, busy or not in a send window); 23% of probed plans reach quorum. In development runs on seeds 1-2 a 96-hour window instead of 48 moved the quorum rate from 5% to 12%, and inviting at least 4 with twice-weekly runs from 12% to 21%; these steps were not re-measured separately at 8 seeds.
- **Capture is the product.** Without the weekly check-in the planner has little to work with (P-std: 3 plan meetings per seed, V14 +2.5). Lower opt-in (10-25%, standing 25%) halves the plan effect (V14 +3.3).
- **Window priming is an assumption, not a finding.** If members who said "free Saturday" answer a matching plan no more readily than a cold probe, plan meetings fall from 12.9 to 4.3 per seed and met + worthwhile gains vanish (V14 still +4.1). This is the first thing a pilot should measure: the yes rate of check-in members to a plan in their stated window.
- **Least misery helps a little:** scoring by the mean adds a plan or two of weaker groups and costs intros slots (-2.3 ± 2.0 met + worthwhile vs P); plan met + worthwhile is the same.
- **The familiarity term costs plan quality here** (-1.6 ± 0.8 plan met + worthwhile per seed vs no term; at 60 days -2.3 ± 2.3). The simulator cannot show its intended benefit (comfort and retention from one known face, which an anonymous probe cannot show before the reveal), and the plan oracle gives a known face only +0.08 x (1 - social energy). It is kept on by default as specified; see the open decision below.
- **Do not hold yes-sayers** (P-hold -2.8 ± 1.4 vs P): a yes waiting for quorum should not block other items; only a booked plan does.
- **Restricting plans to members no intro serves** (design 3.2's D2-D4 framing) halves plan volume and the gain (-2.9 ± 0.7 plan met + worthwhile vs P).

## Defaults (`config.ts DEFAULT_PLANS`, plans-v1.0.0)

- Horizon: starts 72 hours to 7 days ahead; planner runs Monday and Thursday 09:00 local (the pre-weekend run); 6 plans per city per run; one plan per member per run; 5-day member cooldown.
- Demand: a stated (check-in), standing or learned window with P(free) >= 0.5; activity fit 1 (stated interest, skill or want); family-only fit (0.5) does not qualify.
- Group: invite 4-6 (activity-partner plans 2, only when no group clears the floors), quorum 3 (2), beam width 8, 3 alternates; U = 0.6 min + 0.4 mean, familiarity +0.05 / -0.10 (clamped +0.10 / -0.20); floors min u_i >= 0.40, U >= 0.40; venue in a member's area 1, elsewhere 0.85.
- Lifecycle: probe deadline the earlier of 96 hours after the plan is made and 30 hours before the start; late joins until 6 hours before; a yes does not hold the member (`yesHolds: false`); the reveal is the booked plan; fallbacks on (smaller, solo event, next week with +0.1 fit for 10 days).
- Plan item: `plan_probe`, Ê = 0.1 + U, the member's acceptance prior; review before the first probe.
- Stated windows: confidence 0.8, P(free) inside -> 0.85, outside the stated week x 0.6.
- Crews: >= 3 members positive at 2 plans (`crews.minPlans: 2`), weekly, hand-off after 3 sessions.
- `unservedOnly: false`, `partnerPlans: true`.

## Open decisions for the founder

1. **Familiarity term.** Specified and on; measured as a small cost to plan quality in this simulator, which cannot represent its benefit. Recommendation: keep it on for the pilot and measure "came again / would do again" with and without a known face, or set `familiarity.oneBonus` to 0 until that is measured.
2. **Weekly check-in as the main capture.** The plan effect depends on it. It is a profiling ask (never on the cap), opt-in. Recommendation: offer it at onboarding and after a member's first plan.
3. **Crews.** Feed "anyone you'd do this again with?" back as `would_interact_again` edges so the planner regroups people who enjoyed a plan; or offer a crew after one plan with >= 3 positive (`crews.minPlans: 1`). Neither is the default.
4. **Plans compete with intros for the 2/7d cap.** Over 60 days intros lose about a quarter of their meetings. Options: a separate plan allowance (a plan probe not counting against the cap when the member stated the window, like a reply to their own message, 1.1), or lower plan priority for members with an intro in flight. Neither was measured here.

## Integration asks for the network session (packages/network)

1. **Capture.** A member setting "weekly check-in" (opt-in). Send "what's your week like?" Sunday 17:00 local as a profiling ask: `countsAgainstCap: false`, only with `outboundSinceInbound <= 1`, outside quiet hours, never while another reply is awaited. Parse the reply into `plans.StatedWindows` (windows, `until` = end of week) and activity hints. Store standing availability ("usually free Tue evenings") from onboarding as `StandingAvailability`.
2. **Planner run.** Monday and Thursday 09:00 local per city, and after a new stated window: `plans.planProposals(world, { now, city, tz, evidence, venues, busyAt, exclude, lastPlannedAt, carry }, DEFAULT_PLANS)`. `evidence` per member: quiet hours, standing windows, accepted / attended / declined-time history, stated windows; only members with evidence. `busyAt`: booked meetings within 4 hours. `exclude`: members pending or yes in a live plan.
3. **Review.** Each plan (primary group and alternates) is one opportunity in the review queue (`planToProposal`: generator "plan", kind "group" or "intro", category social). Approved before the first probe; backfill from the reviewed alternates needs no new review. Crew: review once when proposed.
4. **Probes and answers.** `planItem` into the hold queue; copy from `buildPlanProbe` (it runs the leak gate; null means do not send). "Yes but not that time" is a no plus a `declined_time` history entry. Drive the lifecycle with `startPlanRun`, `recordPlanAnswer`, `checkPlanDeadline`; actions: book, join, probe_partner, backfill, fallback.
5. **Booking.** The reveal is the booked plan to each member who said yes: names, time, public place, "Everyone pays their own way. Reply if you can't make it." It is logistics (not on the cap; needs `outboundSinceInbound <= 2`). A "can't" removes that member only; cancel below 2. Before booking, drop anyone booked elsewhere within 4 hours. When choosing a time for an intro, treat a member's booked plans as away.
6. **A yes waiting for quorum does not block other items;** report only booked members of a plan as an open opportunity to the engine.
7. **Fallbacks** with `planFallback`: smaller -> book with the yes-sayers; solo event -> an `event_suggestion` item for each yes-sayer; next week -> keep the returned `carry` entries and pass them to the next runs. Tell yes-sayers "that plan didn't come together" in their next message, never who declined.
8. **After the plan:** day-of check-in and the post-plan answers (who came; "anyone you'd do this again with?") -> history `attended`, `would_interact_again` edges, and a `PlanOutcomeRecord` for `detectCrews`; crew sessions with `crewSessionPlan`, opt-in each week, host on the contribution budget, hand-off after 3 sessions.
9. **Venues.** A curated public venue table per city (`Venue`; volunteer organizations with `org`). No home addresses, no deposits, no payments through the Network; `ageMin` enforced.
10. **Judge.** Add invariants: no member under 18 in any plan role; no other invitee named in a plan message before the plan was booked; plan reveals only after quorum. Plan probes carry `meta.probe.key` = plan id; reveals `meta.type: "proposal"`, `meta.proposalId` = plan id, `meta.reveal: true`.

**For the simulator owner (packages/sim):** (a) persona answers to anonymous, time-specific plan probes and to "what's your week like?" (this harness's `planYesProb`, `checkInAnswer` and `hiddenAvailability` are a starting point; the window-priming assumption is the one to calibrate); (b) a plan oracle for group activities (this harness's `planEnjoyment`) so the sim scores plan meetings itself; (c) multi-item replies ("1 and 2"); (d) post-meeting "would do this again" feeding `would_interact_again` edges into the snapshot; (e) time-dependent attendance (fix 3) in the sim; (f) the stub's reminder prints "today at -2pm" for a 10am meeting (`localHour - 12`).
