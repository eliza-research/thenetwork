# Attribute group no-shows to the people reported absent

Updated: 2026-10-30. Base: `a0ed7bc6495d9e524afc3d500738212a28dc7bc2`.
Current code and regression: `0c4fc60bd8e515678e86b1752a5bcc3368e45953`.
Local combined code: `6de053d215ef62ea74237be76d38d344a1399da7`.

## Requirement, problem and cause

Reproduced defect under the approved Slop scope. PRD 15.2 requires contextual
reliability evidence; PRD 28's learning rules require corroborated negative feedback.
A missing reply alone does not identify someone as absent.

Originally, a group report such as "Ben didn't show up" penalized every silent
attendee, including Cara. The initial fix restricted the penalty to named members
and retained the explicit "Nobody else came" behavior.

However, the name list still used the whole reply. "Cara came. Ben didn't show up"
therefore named both people as missing. This affected ordinary group introductions
and planner-generated hikes after the four-day unanswered-check-in window.

## Fix and alternatives

The finalizer requires an identified member for a group absence report. Pairs keep
their existing single-counterpart rule. Explicit everyone-absent reports populate
the existing name list with all counterparts.

When the reply reports someone missing, collect names only from its absence
sentences or contrast clauses. Sentence punctuation, newlines, and `but`, `while`
or `whereas` separate those clauses. Commas stay intact because names can form a list.
The clause check reuses `feedbackOf`, locally normalizing "never came" to its
existing "never come" form. Top-level feedback classification is unchanged.
Replies without an absence report keep their existing name-reading behavior.

Both ordinary and planner feedback use this path. The change adds no storage
field, dependency, message template or client contract. The waiting period,
self-reported absence, direct replies and corroborating positive replies retain
their behavior. The capital ledger's pair-only inference policy is unchanged.

Alternatives considered:

1. Ignore every group absence report. That loses valid named and everyone-absent evidence.
2. Add a second persisted attendance structure. The existing member-ID list already
   survives restarts and can represent the corrected attribution.
3. Split at every comma or `and`. That breaks "Ben, Cara and Dan didn't show up."
   Existing name lists must stay together.

## measurement

The existing gate, `group attendance: a no-show report identifies the silent member`,
was expanded in place. It now covers **18 replies × three workflows × seeds 1, 2, 3 = 162 workflows**.
The workflows are reviewed pairs, reviewed three-person groups, and four-person
hikes generated by the real planner.

Each synthetic workflow onboards adults, obtains simulated staff approval and
member consent, books, delivers feedback questions, and finalizes. Four JSON
restarts check persistence and finalization idempotence. Counterparts receive
no penalty before the waiting window ends. No real messages are sent.

The original 72 cases cover named and unnamed absence, two everyone-absent
phrases, positive feedback, self-reported absence, a disputed report, and a
corroborating reply. All remain unchanged. Ninety additional cases cover seven
ways of separating present and absent attendees, two absence lists, and one
reply with two separate absence sentences.

## Reproduce and limits

1. Check out `afad0656256938f3181fb1d5e440765928031b7a` separately.
2. Load `scripts/sim/network.ts` from `0c4fc60bd8e515678e86b1752a5bcc3368e45953`.
3. Run the affected command. Expect 152/153 and `expected 120 to be 162`.
4. Load `packages/network/src/network.ts` from the same code commit and repeat.
5. Expect 153/153 and 162 correct `GROUP_ABSENCE_OUTCOMES` rows.

First-name matching remains ambiguous for duplicate first names. This does not
interpret quotations, pronouns across sentences, or mixed subjects within a clause
joined only by a comma or `and`. It does not infer positive attendance credit from
every present-person clause or broaden top-level absence classification.

Already-finalized penalties are not repaired. Older pending reports retain their
stored name lists; their original text is not kept, so those identities cannot be
recomputed. No live sends or deployment. Draft pending review and hosted CI.
