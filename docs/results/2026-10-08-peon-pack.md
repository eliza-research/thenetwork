## Official gates (adopted 2026-10-08)

The founder adopted the replacement gates proposed in iteration 1 (section 6) and iteration 2 (I2.5). They are the official launch gates for peon.biz, implemented in `packages/sim/src/apps/peon/gates.ts` (`officialGates`) and declared in `peonPack.metrics.gates`. The CLI prints them for any arm (`--gate-arm`). Each compares the pack with the keyword job board and the hidden-truth oracle **on the same seeds**:

| # | Official gate | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hires ≥ 0.9x keyword, with ≤ 1/5 of its intros | Hires are supply-limited in a 400 x 80 pilot (the oracle reaches about 1.2x); peon must not lose hires while cutting volume |
| 2 | Hires ≥ 0.8x oracle | Distance to the upper bound |
| 3 | Interviews per hire ≤ 0.75x keyword | Recruiter and candidate time (relative: the absolute ≤ 12 passes trivially at pilot scale) |
| 4 | 90-day retention within 2 points of keyword, or better | Retention is about 95-98% in every arm; the SE is about 1.5 points |
| 5 | Under-applied hires ≥ 0.9x keyword, and a Gini of applications per job below keyword's | Non-inferiority on under-applied roles, plus less concentration |
| 6 | Impact ratio ≥ 0.8 for every sealed group at the automated stage (pooled over seeds) | Four-fifths rule at the stage the tool decides |
| 7 | Median reach per scam employer that got an intro ≤ 1 | The domain research's scam gate (B5) |
| 8 | Minors matched = 0; unverified-employer intros = 0; intros to jobs without a pay range = 0 | Core and PRD 40.8 |

**Historical gates** (the first proposal, kept for the record and printed beside the official ones):

- hires ≥ 2x keyword;
- interviews per hire ≤ 12;
- retention ≥ keyword;
- impact ratio ≥ 0.8;
- scam reach ≤ 1 per seed;
- under-applied ≥ 1.5x keyword;
- 0 minors.

The 2x and 1.5x gates are above what the oracle reaches in this world.

**Current status (Iteration 3, held-out seeds 13-16): every official gate passes.** Checks after rebasing onto `origin/main` at the time: `bunx tsc --noEmit -p .` was clean, and the engine and worlds test suites gave 421 pass, 0 fail (now validated by `bun run sim`). No LLM calls. Gate 3 passes at its threshold: 0.748, against 0.750 on the tuning seeds 1-12. The historical 2x hires and 1.5x under-applied gates still fail.

## Iteration 3: deadline-aware pacing (tuned on seeds 1-12, held out on seeds 13-16)

The founder adopted the replacement gates (above). The branch was rebased onto `origin/main` (`70c988b`, which includes the peon merge `6a62e55`, the LA type fix `3df1d3d` and slopPack iteration 2). Then:

- the official gates went into code (`gates.ts`, `peonPack.metrics.gates`, the CLI);
- the pack got deadline-aware pacing.

### I3.1 What changed

**World.** Employers state a fill-by date at job intake: posting date + 8, 6 or 4 weeks by urgency, the same deadline the world enforces. It is in the snapshot as `peon:fill_by:<epoch day>` (matchable), grounded in domain research B6 ("interview steps and timeline"). Fills through other channels (4% a week) stay invisible.

**Pack (`selection.ts`).**

- **closeness** = 1 - weeks left before the fill-by date / 8, clamped to [0, 1]. The window is 8 weeks because a pipeline takes 2-3, so a 4-week urgent role starts at closeness 0.5.
- **Larger, sooner slates.** A role's yes-slate target is (2 + openings) x (1 + closeness), with the maximum raised from 5 by up to 4. A role close to its date therefore gets more probes in its first week, not only near the end.
- **Urgent verified roles on the new-employer limit** get 10 probes a week (split across the company's open seats) instead of 3. Urgent means urgency 3.
- **Not adopted:** a selection-value lift for deadline closeness (`pacingLift`). It did not help on seeds 1-8 or 1-12, and stays 0.

### I3.6 Remaining risks

- **Relaxing the new-employer limit trades scam exposure for hires.** On these 16 seeds, the one or two scams per seed that pass verification without a cue each reached at most 1 candidate. If scammers learn to claim urgency, the relaxation is the first thing to revisit: it applies only to urgency 3, which the employer states themselves.
- **Interviews per hire sits at the gate's threshold** (I3.4).
- **Assumptions:** the world's deadlines, fatigue, trust and recruiter-time parameters (I2.1), the relay screen's recall (0.7), and the cue rates.
