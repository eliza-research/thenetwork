# Distinguish age clauses from scores and measurements

The #444 contribution found that both readers could misread a low sports score as an age or overlook an explicit fractional age. This port applies the approved fractional and conjunction rule to the shared readers and keeps the original contributor commit in the history. An explicit current fractional age can confirm an under-13 refusal. Loose age evidence can still reach the existing age-floor and staff-review path. [#444](https://github.com/eliza-research/thenetwork/pull/444) · [Approved batch rule](https://github.com/eliza-research/thenetwork/pull/581)

The first alternative was to treat every fractional reading as an age. That would misread measurements and allow bare ages below eight to be read from heights. The second was to ignore every fractional reading until a second turn. That would miss a member who already gave a current age in explicit words. The shared decision uses the caller's observed spelling, units, and clause context.
