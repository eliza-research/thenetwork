# Product policies

Use these rules in [member and staff stories](stories.md). The [pilot plan](pilot.md) defines release and operating criteria.

## Age, identity and enrollment

- A verified phone resolves to one internal person. Membership, profile, sessions, agent grants and app consent stay bound to one app. Phone changes preserve the person, the history and authorized grants. A recycled number is held for review when ownership is uncertain. A banned phone cannot join, authenticate or resume through another app.
- Use the lowest stated or recorded age at the person level. Under 13, refuse the join and keep no profile or membership. At 13–17, allow personal help only. An unknown age fails adult gates. Check age before retrieval, before contact, at acceptance and dispatch, at reveal, during relay, and before fetching or rating a photo. A later age downgrade ends pending multi-person participation and removes age-ineligible data.
- Each public site directs the person to their agent or shows an independent Messages entry. Sign-in UI belongs only to an authorized signup or connector flow. slop, friends and ntwrk direct signup remains in the current agent, uses app-bound HTTPS, checks current app and consent, verifies the person's genuine texted code with a private session, and reads the result back. It requires the agent's existing capabilities. A capability failure ends the flow before credentials are collected. An invite-only app still requires its invite.
- Hosted connectors that are authorized use OAuth 2.1 with human phone confirmation, app scope, expiry and revocation. Their tools authorize on the server. Separate private adult Slop connector qualification does not publish Slop as a public ChatGPT connector. Use the [app signup skills](../../sites/skills/) for the exact active app transport. Peon's legacy skill describes an earlier browser/MCP path. Its active entry and transport require separate qualification.
- Existing eliza.app users receive one takeover notice before normal join and consent. A shared-line no-keyword join asks whether they want friends, dating or work. Each app membership needs its own consent. A known number's join gives no information about other apps.

## Interruption and delivery

- The engine reads time from its Clock and randomness from its seeded generator. Stable ordering makes decisions replayable. Member data never includes a simulator's hidden facts. Audit records identify the app and store the reason for decisions, retries, expiry and suppression.
- Check membership, age, bans, holds, blocks, category consent, participation, quiet hours, deadlines, state and budget at the relevant send boundary. Every outbound text passes a leak check. Replies to paused or restricted people are allowed within consent and safety policy. Safety and account notices use the compliance path. Consent handling takes priority over unresolved conversation.
- On the shared Blooio line, STOP and STOP ALL stop every app, leave removes only the named app, START resumes the allowed app, and HELP works as a compliance reply. Consent uses event time so a delayed older START cannot undo a newer STOP. An eligible person who texts after STOP can receive at most one brief resume hint per number and app in 30 days. The gateway mirrors the service's decision into its dispatch fence and sends the one confirmation. A collected turn or provider acceptance is recorded once under its message identity.
- Normal allows two new opportunity interruptions per 7 days, Open four, Quiet one per 30 days, Receiving support only, Paused or Only when asked zero. The cap counts the first invite to each person, including their partner probe and a menu of 1–3 items as one message. Replies and logistics after consent do not spend this invite cap. A separate plan allowance allows at most one initial plan invite per 7 days. Apply the stricter of the participation-state cap and one solo offer per 7 days for minors. The person-level maximum is 3 proactive messages a day across apps. See [participation budgets](../participationBudgets.md).
- Default rolling delivery time is 12:00 local, then adjusted from replies. Learned signals may make outreach quieter. Unanswered outreach counts at expiry or 72 hours, whichever is first. Two unanswered invitations pause new offers. A single high-value re-engagement after at least 30 days must still obey the carrier rule. Apply the carrier streak, caps and quiet hours to agent-started logistics too. A proactive message that waits a full day expires unsent. Other queued message kinds expire after three days. Qualify the currently configured line limits of 20 new conversations and 200 agent-started messages per day through the pilot.
- Outbound queues persist through crashes and duplicate provider deliveries. Re-evaluate dispatch when work resumes, and retain the terminal outcome. Reuse the same request ID when a provider or gateway reports an unknown delivery. Notify uses one inbox across surfaces, rechecks whether an item was seen, and may bundle at most one message per person per send. A link back to the person's agent carries a task reference only.

## Matching, consent and plans

- App packs may tighten common policy. Apply hard age, consent, safety, geography, category and capacity filters before scoring. A model judge can veto an eligible proposal. Rank by mutual fit, evidence, timing, role coverage, travel, effort and load. Penalize repetition and concentration. Log score components and build reasons from shareable evidence. Collect outcomes and reviewer labels without discarding learned state on profile changes.
- Human review covers every proactive proposal and member-initiated request before contact. Reviewers can approve, edit, swap a participant, re-roll or reject with a reason. A materially changed candidate or message returns to the appropriate checks. Never send a proposal past its SLA. An explicit yes to a reviewed anonymous probe is the only yes that advances it. A counter, hedge, silence or expired answer gives no consent.
- Probes give an activity or intent, time or effort, a coarse area, and at most one shareable fact. Omit names, employers, phone numbers, exact private locations and photos. Ask the person with the live want first for pairs. Only then ask the other person independently. Groups use the approved quorum and alternates. A decline or expiry tells other people neither who declined nor why.
- The reveal follows independent consent. The agent confirms a shared slot or presents 2–3 options. Slop uses a short public first date. For group plans, choose an activity and public venue around overlapping availability and travel tolerance. A material reschedule requires renewed consent. Release failed quorum or cancelled plans without blame. A good plan can become a recurring crew with a rotating host and per-session opt-in.
- Lower-confidence items wait only while useful. Keep a bounded hold queue, at most 10 items per member, with expiry and a fresh eligibility check before use. Offer held items on request to people who accept only requested help. When a live intent has no match, diagnose missing data, missing intent, thin supply, a blocked format, or budget, and offer the relevant outside-world or other permitted alternative.
- Slop applies mutual stated preferences for gender/seeking, age range, intent, distance and dealbreakers. Keep gender, self-description and seeking separate. When a woman or man stays unclear after two questions, read back the founder's default and allow correction. Never default for a nonbinary person. No inferred orientation, race or ethnicity fields, health, immigration, or information from another app enters matching. Use reciprocal scoring, congestion limits and exposure floors. At most one first date is in scheduling at a time and no new probe goes out while a mutual yes waits on that member.
- Slop photos may be rated after adult eligibility and consent. Clef ratings are private soft matching inputs and unavailable to members, staff views, explanations, logs and relay copy. Ratings are on by default using placeholder weights until P2 qualifies fitted weights. Monitor ratios by group and rating quintile weekly. The only member-to-member photo share is a member's choice inside a consented relay.
- Friends uses public activity-first groups of 3–6 with quorum and repeat sessions. Enforce affinity choices, travel tolerance, alcohol-venue ages and the friends-only purpose. Peon uses candidate-first consent, verified employers, visible pay ranges, current-employer blocks, and unranked slates of 3–5. Exclude protected attributes and proxies from automated matching and judge input. A human hiring team owns hiring decisions.

## Relay, safety and privacy

- After consent, relay a member's own selected text or item only inside an eligible open match. Each item is independently consented. Contact exchange is optional and requires both sides' agreement. A safety hold, block, STOP, leave, age downgrade or closed match ends affected relay access. Before dispatch, recheck pair, item, sender, recipient and current consent. Confirmed or queued delivery is distinct from an allowed moderation decision.
- Use deterministic relay policy followed by Clef for scam and harassment classification. Clef may escalate. Missing production classifier support holds otherwise passable text for staff. Use only the rendered, leak-checked relay text at delivery. A minimal log records the decision and delivery evidence. An item approval reveals only the information approved for that item.
- Use confirmed sources and record provenance, scope and confidence. Member-private inference never appears in generated probes, summaries, explanations or model inputs that lack authorization. No scraped non-member profiles, imported contacts as people, or sensitive-trait inference. Optional imports use the member's own AI summary, pasted LinkedIn profile, approved calendar free/busy, or another permitted source. Delete raw pasted summaries after confirmation. Use only allowed public event feeds and fresh sources for concierge facts.
- An immediate block keeps the pair apart everywhere. A report preserves necessary case evidence and enters safety triage. Urgent cases target 1 hour and ordinary cases 24 hours. A hold stops matching. A ban rejects future joins and authentication. High-risk proposals follow the scope limits in [F14](stories.md#story-f14--ask-for-bounded-help) and [deferred scope](README.md#deferred-scope).
- Encrypt sensitive data in transit and at rest. Share only coarse location or travel bands. Keep logs and analytics scrubbed. Staff sign in with a second factor, receive app-scoped roles, and use the staff API for actions. Authorized safety/admin PII reveals apply to one member, require a reason, expire after 15 minutes and are audited. Audit every sensitive staff read and cross-app access. The engineer role works with simulated people only.
- Export and deletion include derived state where feasible. Deletion closes open work, erases derived embeddings and app data, and preserves only required suppression and safety records under their retention policy. Keep purge and backup windows documented and replay erasures after a restore. See [SEC-001 through SEC-007](source/prd.md#226-security-requirements).

## Network capital and success

- Keep a private internal ledger from verified vouches, attendance, feedback, recipient-confirmed help and organizing. Give everyone a high baseline for agent effort and use diminishing returns. Ledger entries may affect effort, vouch capacity, organizing reach and staff review context. They never buy access to a person or boost someone else's view of the member.
- Declining, sharing more data, inactivity, quiet and pause do not affect capital. One confirmed no-show is forgiven. Vouch liability is bounded to serious confirmed abuse in the defined window. Record contextual reliability separately. Exclude minors from the ledger and allow the adult member to read an itemized history in conversation.
- Value every 14 days (V14) measures the share of eligible active members with at least one value event in the last 14 days. Eligible members have at least 14 days of tenure and are not paused. An acted-on concierge answer, booked or completed activity, or helpful response can count as defined by the metric. Measure useful outcomes, repeat relationships, load and safety. Keep messages, time in app and raw proposal volume as diagnostic counts.
