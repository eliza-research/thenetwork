## Founder decision — 2026-11-16: reciprocal matching, lanes and bounded search

1. **Goal.** Match people with people who are close to them in interests and in approximate value to each other, and build circles of people who fit. A match must be a good value exchange in both directions. Do not propose a pair with a large standing gap: for example, do not introduce a well-known investor to a first-time founder as peers. The same rule applies to appearance and to money. Keep the engine clean, concise and verified, with minimal bloat.
2. **One engine, seven lanes.** A lane is a relationship type. One generic engine serves all four apps. The apps differ only by the lanes they offer and by feature extraction. [Relationship lanes](../../matchLane.md) holds the one lane table: `Friend` (friends, ntwrk), `Date` (slop), `Cofounder` (peon, ntwrk), `Intro` (ntwrk), `Hire` (peon), `Mentor` (ntwrk) and `Invest` (ntwrk). `Friend`, `Date`, `Cofounder` and `Intro` pair peers. `Hire`, `Mentor` and `Invest` pair an offering side with a seeking side. Only `Mentor` intends a standing offset: the mentor sits above the mentee by consent. Each number in the table is a named decision and changes only with measured evidence.
3. **Reciprocal pair value.** [Reciprocal pair value](../../pairValue.md) gives a pair a value only when both members reach the lane's utility floor, the standing gap, after the lane's intended offset, stays inside the lane's band plus both uncertainties, and, where the lane requires it, the exchange is balanced. The value is the harmonic mean of the two utilities less a gap penalty.
4. **Private standing.** [Private member standing](../../memberStanding.md) holds each member's standing per lane. Standing is never shown to any member, never appears in explanations, probes or relay text, and never reads acceptance, reply or popularity counts. A new member gets the widest uncertainty, which widens and never narrows the band of members the engine considers. Standing exists only for an adult with the lane's consent.
5. **Bounded and exact candidate search.** The engine does not score every member against every other member. [Bounded candidate search](../../candidateSearch.md) limits the pair checks for each member to the lane's search budget. A search that closes as exact returns the same best candidates as a full scan. A search that stops at its budget reports a bound on the value it may have missed.
6. **Circles.** [Group formation](../../groupFormation.md) admits a circle only when every pair in it has a real pair value at the lane's circle floor and the standing spread stays inside the lane's band plus the members' uncertainties. `Friend` and `Intro` form circles.
7. **Name.** The work app stays peon.biz.
8. **Open founder decisions.** (a) Mentor circles: a mentee-to-mentee pair has no pair value, so the `Mentor` lane forms no circles now. A circle around one offering member needs its own admission rule; the founder decides whether to add it. (b) Hire level: a role level in the `Hire` lane is a staff-reviewed property of the posting, and the level gap is a pass-or-fail filter that never orders a slate. Seniority can act as a proxy for age, so the founder decides whether the level filter stays.

## Platform decisions (founder, 2026-10-08)
