Safety notice

Last changed October 8, 2026. This notice is information for you. You do not have to accept it to join.

We do not run background checks

slop.date does not conduct criminal background checks or identity checks on its members. We cannot promise that a member is who they say they are, or that they have no criminal record. Use your own judgement every time.

Before you meet

  • Keep talking through slop.date until you feel comfortable. You do not have to share your number.
  • Do not share your home address, your workplace, your daily routine, or financial details.
  • Never send money, gift cards or crypto to someone you met here, whatever the story. Report anyone who asks.

When you meet

  • Meet in a busy public place. Stay in public for the first few dates.
  • Tell a friend where you are going, who you are meeting, and when you expect to be home.
  • Get there and home on your own. Keep your phone charged.
  • Watch your drink. Do not leave it alone. Drink only as much as you are comfortable with.
  • Leave whenever you want. You do not owe anyone an explanation.

Sexual health and consent

  • Consent is required every time, for everything, and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Protect your sexual health. Talk about testing and protection before you are intimate.

If something goes wrong

  • If you are in danger, call 911.
  • Text slop.date to block or report someone, or email help@ntwrk.party. A person reviews every report. When you block someone, they are blocked for you in every service Eliza Research Corporation runs.
  • Safe Horizon runs a free, confidential 24-hour hotline in New York City for people affected by violence or abuse: 1-800-621-4673.
  • The National Sexual Assault Hotline (RAINN): 1-800-656-4673.
