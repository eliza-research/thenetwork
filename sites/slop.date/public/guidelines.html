Community guidelines

Dating is awkward enough. These rules keep slop.date kind and safe.

Be real

  • Use your real first name, your real age and recent details.
  • Be honest about what you want, even if it is "not sure yet".

Consent, always

  • A no, or no answer, is the end of it. Do not ask again through someone else.
  • No sexual messages or photos the other person did not clearly ask for.
  • Do not pressure anyone to drink, to go somewhere private, or to share their address.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, threats, hate, or stalking.
  • Selling anything, sex work, or asking for money.
  • Fake profiles, scams, or pretending to be someone else.
  • Anyone under 18.

Block and report

Text slop.date to block or report someone, or email help@slop.date. A person reviews every report. We may pause or remove a member while we look into it. If you are in danger, call 911 first.

slop.date is operated by Eliza Research Corporation. Questions: help@slop.date. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop and HELP for help.
