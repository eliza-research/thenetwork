What you get, and what you don't

You get

  • One introduction at a time, with a short note on why.
  • A first date plan at a public place, if you both want one.
  • Your number stays private until you both choose to share it.
  • A person on our team checks every introduction before we send it.

You don't get

  • A feed of faces to swipe.
  • Read receipts, streaks, or "someone liked you, pay to see who".
  • Texts at 2 a.m. We keep quiet hours.
  • Your details shown to anyone you did not say yes to.

How it works

  1. Join here. Your number, a code, your first name and age. Two minutes.
  2. Text with the matchmaker. Tell it who you want to meet and what you're done with. That stays between you and slop.date.
  3. Say yes or no. When the other person says yes too, we help you pick a time and a public place.

We are not sending introductions yet. Join now, and the matchmaker texts you when introductions start in your area.

    Before you meet anyone

    slop.date does not run criminal background checks or identity checks on members. We cannot promise that a person is who they say they are.

    • Meet in a busy public place. Get there and home on your own.
    • Tell a friend where you are going and who you are meeting.
    • Never send money or financial details to someone you met here.
    • Leave whenever you want. Text us to block or report anyone.
