Good to know before you meet anyone

slop.date does not run criminal background checks or identity checks on members. We cannot promise that a person is who they say they are, or that they have no criminal record.

  • Meet in a busy public place. Get there and home on your own.
  • Tell a friend where you are going and who you are meeting.
  • Keep your home address, workplace and money details to yourself.
  • Leave whenever you want. Text us to block or report anyone.
