Support

How to get help with The Network.

This page covers The Network and every app it powers: slop.date, friends.help and peon.biz. All of these apps are powered by The Network.

Email
help@ntwrk.love. A person reads every message. Never send us a login code.
By text
Text HELP to our number for help. Text STOP to stop every text from us, for The Network and every app it powers. Text "leave" and the app name, for example "leave slop.date" to stop one app only. Text START to resume.
Your account
On the settings page you can see your membership, export your data, stop messages, leave, or delete everything tied to your number.
Safety
Text us to block or report someone, or email us. A person reviews every report. If you are in danger, call 911 first.
AI assistants
Assistants can read our skill file. An assistant may explain The Network and give you a join link. It must never sign you up, ask for your login code or type it. You confirm your own phone number on our page.
Policies
Privacy policy, terms, text messaging terms and community guidelines.
