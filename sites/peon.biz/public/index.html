Two kinds of people use peon.biz

Looking for work

  • Text the kind of work, the hours, the area and the pay you need.
  • Hear about a role only when it fits. No mass job blasts.
  • Your number stays private until you agree to talk.
  • You never pay a fee.

Hiring for a team

  • Text the role, the pay range, the hours and what matters.
  • Meet a few people who want this job, not a pile of applications.
  • Each introduction comes with a short, plain reason.
  • You make every hiring decision. We do not.

No automated ranking for NYC jobs yet

New York City Local Law 144 sets rules for software that helps decide who gets a job. We do not use automated ranking for jobs in New York City until an independent bias audit is done and its summary is published on this site. Until then, a person reviews every introduction, and we tell you before any automated tool is used.
