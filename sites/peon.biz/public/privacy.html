Privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026.

peon.biz offers introductions by text between people looking for work and teams that are hiring. This policy says what we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your phone number, to check that it is yours and to text you.
  • Your short profile: first name, age, area (neighbourhood or ZIP code), interests and one line about you.
  • What you tell the peon.biz agent by text.
  • Work details you tell the agent: the work you want or the role you are filling, skills, schedule, area and pay range.
  • Activity: introductions or plans offered, accepted or declined, and feedback you give.
  • Your consent: the exact opt-in wording you agreed to, with the date and time.

We do not collect device location. We do not build profiles of people who are not members.

How we use it

  • To keep your membership, and later to suggest introductions between candidates and teams. A person reviews every introduction. We never use race, religion, health, immigration status or other protected traits to choose introductions.
  • To text you, relay messages, and send reminders you asked for.
  • To keep members safe: reports, blocks, and stopping spam and abuse.

Who can see what

Other members see only what you agree to share, and only a coarse area, never your exact location. Your phone number is shared with another member only when you both agree. A small team reviews introductions before we send them. Reviewers see what they need to judge the fit, without your phone number.

Kept apart from our other services

Eliza Research Corporation runs more than one service. Your peon.biz data stays in peon.biz. We do not use it in another service. We never tell anyone whether a phone number uses another of our services. If you block someone, we block them for you in every service we run. Your age is one fact about you: if you give different ages, we use the lowest one.

Service providers

We use messaging providers, cloud hosting and database providers, and AI model providers that process message text. They process data only on our instructions. We do not sell your personal information. Mobile numbers and opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing.

Your choices

  • Export this site's data about you on the settings page.
  • Stop messages by replying STOP, or on the settings page.
  • Leave peon.biz on the settings page. We delete your peon.biz profile, messages and matching data.
  • Delete everything on the settings page. We delete all data tied to your phone number in every service we run. We keep a scrambled one-way copy of the number so that we never text it again, and records the law requires us to keep.

We delete data from storage within 24 hours, and from encrypted backups when they expire, within 30 days. Depending on where you live, including California and New York, you may have more rights. We will not treat you differently for using them. Email help@ntwrk.party.

Age

You must be 13 or older to join. Members aged 13 to 17 can use the agent for their own questions only. peon.biz never matches them or introduces them to anyone. Only adults 18 and older are matched. If you tell us different ages, we use the lowest one. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if we learn of any, we delete it.

Security and changes

We encrypt data in transit and at rest, and we limit and log staff access. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires. We will text you before a material change to this policy takes effect.

peon.biz is powered by The Network and operated by Eliza Research Corporation. Help: support or help@ntwrk.party. Message and data rates may apply. Text STOP to stop all texts from us, or "leave peon.biz" to stop peon.biz only. Text HELP for help.
