On the settings page you can see your membership, export your data, stop messages, leave, or delete everything tied to your number.
Safety
Text us to block or report someone, or email us. A person reviews every report. If you are in danger, call 911 first.

Assistants can read our skill file. An assistant may explain peon.biz and give you a join link. It must never sign you up, ask for your login code or type it. You confirm your own phone number on our page.
