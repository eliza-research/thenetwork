Text messaging terms

Draft. Not yet in effect. Last changed October 8, 2026.

  • Program: peon.biz sends conversational text messages (SMS and iMessage): questions about the work you want or the role you are filling, waitlist updates, introductions you agree to, interview scheduling and reminders.
  • How you opt in: on the join page, you enter your number, confirm it with a code, and tick a box that says: "I agree to receive recurring text messages from peon.biz at this number: questions about work or hiring, waitlist updates, introductions I agree to, and scheduling reminders. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase." We record that wording with the date and time.
  • Consent is not a condition of any purchase.
  • Frequency: message frequency varies with how you use peon.biz. Unprompted messages are limited (two a week by default), and we do not text during quiet hours in your time zone.
  • Stop: reply STOP to stop messages from peon.biz. You get one confirmation and nothing else. Reply STOP ALL to stop messages from every service Eliza Research Corporation runs at your number. You can also stop messages on the settings page.
  • Start again: reply START.
  • Help: reply HELP, or email help@peon.biz.
  • Costs: message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Privacy: Mobile numbers and opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing. See the privacy policy.

peon.biz is operated by Eliza Research Corporation. Questions: help@peon.biz. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop and HELP for help.
