Text messaging terms

Draft. Not yet in effect. Last changed October 8, 2026.

  • Program: buddies.nyc sends conversational text messages (SMS and iMessage): questions about what you like to do, invitations to small group plans you can accept or skip, plan details, reminders and check-ins.
  • How you opt in: on the join page, you enter your number, confirm it with a code, and tick a box that says: "I agree to receive recurring text messages from buddies.nyc at this number: questions about what I like to do, invitations to plans, plan details and reminders. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase." We record that wording with the date and time.
  • Consent is not a condition of any purchase.
  • Frequency: message frequency varies with how you use buddies.nyc. Unprompted messages are limited (two a week by default), and we do not text during quiet hours in your time zone.
  • Stop: reply STOP to stop messages from buddies.nyc. You get one confirmation and nothing else. Reply STOP ALL to stop messages from every service Eliza Research Corporation runs at your number. You can also stop messages on the settings page.
  • Start again: reply START.
  • Help: reply HELP, or email help@buddies.nyc.
  • Costs: message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Privacy: Mobile numbers and opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing. See the privacy policy.

buddies.nyc is operated by Eliza Research Corporation. Questions: help@buddies.nyc. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop and HELP for help.
