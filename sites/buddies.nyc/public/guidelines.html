Community guidelines

buddies.nyc is for making friends. Keep it friendly, public and easy to say no to.

Plans

  • Plans happen at public places: parks, cafés, courts, museums, bars with a crowd.
  • Show up if you said yes. If you can't, say so early.
  • Small groups are the default. Nobody has to bring anyone.

Friends first

  • This is not a dating service. Do not use plans to hit on people.
  • A no, or no answer, is the end of it.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, threats, hate, or unwanted sexual messages.
  • Selling, recruiting, or promoting to other members.
  • Anyone under 18.

Meet safely

  • Meet in public. Tell a friend where you are going.
  • We do not run background checks on members.
  • Leave whenever you want. You do not owe anyone an explanation.

Block and report

Text buddies.nyc to block or report someone, or email help@buddies.nyc. A person reviews every report. If you are in danger, call 911 first.

buddies.nyc is operated by Eliza Research Corporation. Questions: help@buddies.nyc. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop and HELP for help.
