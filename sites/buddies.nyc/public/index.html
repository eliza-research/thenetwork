How it works

1

Join here

Your number, a code, your first name, age, neighbourhood and what you like to do.

2

Get a plan by text

The agent finds a small group with the same interest, at a time that works, near you. A person on our team checks every plan first.

3

Say yes or skip

When enough people say yes, you get the place and time. Skipping is fine. Nobody sees who skipped.

Only public places

Every plan happens somewhere public, with other people around. Your phone number stays private. Other people see your first name and your neighbourhood, nothing more.

  • Parks
  • Courts and fields
  • Cafés
  • Museums
  • Libraries
  • Bars with a crowd
  • Food halls
  • Running paths

A replay from a test run

The people and messages are made up. We use simulated runs to test buddies.nyc before real people use it.

    Friends, not dates

    buddies.nyc is for making friends. If someone uses a plan to hit on people, tell us. Read the community guidelines. We do not run background checks on members, so meet in public and trust your judgement.
