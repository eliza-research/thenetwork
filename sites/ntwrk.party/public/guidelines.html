Community guidelines

The Network works because members trust each other. These rules keep it that way.

Be who you say you are

  • Use your real first name and your real age.
  • Invite only people you know and would vouch for.

Respect a no

  • Introductions happen only when both people say yes. A no, or no answer, is the end of it.
  • Do not contact a member outside The Network unless they gave you their details.

Meet safely

  • Meet in a public place the first time. Tell a friend where you are going.
  • We do not run background checks on members.
  • Leave whenever you feel uncomfortable. You do not owe anyone an explanation.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, threats, hate, or sexual messages that the other person did not ask for.
  • Selling, recruiting or promoting to other members without their agreement.
  • Contact of any kind with members under 18. The Network never introduces them to anyone.
  • Trying to find out private facts about other members.

Block and report

Text The Network to block or report someone, or email help@ntwrk.party. A person reviews every report. We may pause or remove a member while we look into it. If you are in danger, call 911 first.
