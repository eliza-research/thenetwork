How to join

The Network is invite-only. There is no sign-up form.

  • A member who knows you, or our team, sends you an invitation by text.
  • Reply to that text to accept. Your reply is your opt-in to messages from The Network.
  • You must be 13 or older. Only adults 18 and older are matched. Members under 18 get personal help only. They are never introduced to other members.

Do you know a member? Ask them to invite you.

Already a member? Log in with your phone number to see your membership, export your data, stop messages, or delete your data.
