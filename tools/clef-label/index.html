Before you start: consent and handling rules
  • Adults only. Every photo must be of a verified adult (18+). If anyone in a photo might be under 18, press F and flag it; do not label it.
  • Consented photos only. Only photos whose subject signed a release for use in rating research (listed in the consent manifest). Never scraped, social-media or member photos without that release.
  • Labels stay local. This page makes no network request (its security policy forbids it). Photos never leave this computer; labels are saved as a file you hand to the operator directly, never by email, chat or a shared drive.
  • Raters are adults on the founding team or paid raters who signed the rater agreement. Your rater id is a pseudonym, not your name.
  • Never share or discuss a rating, a ranking or a photo outside the labelling session. Delete your copy of the photos and the labels file when the operator confirms receipt.
  • Judge only the photo. Choose quickly; if you genuinely can't tell, press S to skip.
